Maintenance mode

When on, users see a "come back soon" screen and can't earn, spend or withdraw. Support tickets and offerwall postbacks still work. The people you add below keep using the bot normally, so you can test your update.

Hide my profile details

When on, anyone who taps your name on the Leaderboard sees only your photo, cover, name, AP and UP. Everything else (joined date, streak, ads, referrals, withdrawals...) stays hidden. Your name, AP and UP still show on the Leaderboard and in Payouts.
